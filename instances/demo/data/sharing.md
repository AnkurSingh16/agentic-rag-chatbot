# Sharing notes

> Synthetic demo content. Lumen Notes is a fictional product.

## Share a link

Open the note, choose **Share**, then **Create link**. Anyone with the link can read the note. Links can be set to expire after 1, 7 or 30 days.

## Invite collaborators

On the Plus and Team plans you can invite people by email to edit a note together. Collaborators see each other's cursors while editing.

## Stop sharing

Choose **Share > Manage access** and remove the link or the collaborator. A removed link stops working immediately.
