# Working offline

> Synthetic demo content. Lumen Notes is a fictional product.

Lumen Notes works without a network connection. You can create, edit and delete notes offline; the changes are kept on the device and sync when it reconnects.

## What does not work offline

- Search only covers notes already downloaded to the device.
- Sharing links cannot be created or opened.
- Attachments larger than 10 MB that were never opened on this device are not available.

## Making notebooks available offline

On mobile, notebooks are downloaded on demand. To keep a notebook on the device, open its menu and switch on **Keep offline**.
