# Plans and billing

> Synthetic demo content. Lumen Notes is a fictional product, and these plans and prices are invented.

## Plans

| Plan | Price            | Devices   | Storage |
| ---- | ---------------- | --------- | ------- |
| Free | 0                | 2         | 1 GB    |
| Plus | 4 credits/month  | Unlimited | 50 GB   |
| Team | 8 credits/member | Unlimited | 200 GB  |

## Changing plan

Go to **Settings > Account > Plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Cancelling

Cancel from **Settings > Account > Plan > Cancel plan**. Your notes stay available on the Free plan, but devices beyond the first two stop syncing.
